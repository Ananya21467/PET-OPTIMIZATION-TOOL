Common Diseases & Care

⚠️ When Should You Visit a Vet?

Seek veterinary help if your pet has difficulty breathing, collapses, has seizures, experiences severe bleeding, cannot urinate, or may have swallowed something poisonous.

Repeated vomiting, persistent diarrhoea, loss of appetite, unusual weakness or sudden behaviour changes also need prompt veterinary advice.

Important: This page provides general educational information, not a diagnosis. Do not give human medicines or start treatment without veterinary guidance.
