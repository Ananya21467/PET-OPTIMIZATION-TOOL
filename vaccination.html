2. Common Vaccines for Dogs and Cats

Core vaccines protect against important diseases. Other vaccines may be considered according to local disease risks, lifestyle and veterinary advice.

Important: Vaccine availability, schedules and legal requirements vary by country and region. Do not choose or administer vaccines based on this page alone.

3. Diseases Vaccination Helps Prevent

Dogs and cats

Rabies

A serious viral disease affecting the nervous system. It can spread to humans through infected animal bites. Follow local rabies vaccination requirements.

Dogs

Canine Parvovirus

A contagious disease that can cause severe vomiting, diarrhoea and dehydration, particularly in puppies.

Dogs

Canine Distemper

A contagious viral disease that may affect the respiratory, digestive and nervous systems.

Cats

Feline Panleukopenia

A serious contagious disease that can cause fever, vomiting, diarrhoea and a low white blood cell count.

Cats

Feline Herpesvirus and Calicivirus

Common causes of feline respiratory illness, including sneezing, eye discharge and mouth ulcers.

Risk-dependent

Other Infections

Depending on the animal and region, additional vaccines may be appropriate. Ask your veterinarian about individual exposure risks.

Vaccination reduces the risk of specific infections but does not guarantee complete protection. If your pet is unwell, seek veterinary advice instead of relying on vaccination information.
